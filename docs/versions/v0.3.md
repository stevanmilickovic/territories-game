# v0.3 plan

The goal is to make the world map the starting point for every fight. Players can paint a faction flag, attack or join a named province directly from the map, and fight in a larger version of that province's shape with the first set of pixel art.

## Steps

1. **Redraw the provinces.** Turn the stair-stepped borders and coastlines of the seed `202` map into gently curving vector paths. Keep prominent bends and some sharp turns so the borders still look rugged. Give all ten provinces distinct names.
2. **Put the map controls beside the map.** Keep the square map centered. Split the space on each side into equal halves. Use the inner halves for game controls and information, and reserve the outer halves for future ads. Move the Territories Game title to the left and put the selected province panel below it. Remove the Territories button and list. Show province names, faction ownership colors, and a visual marker for active battles on the map. Keep the yellow hover outline and replace the white selection square with a slightly thicker white outline that follows the province border.
3. **Add the faction flag editor.** Rename **Factions** to **Faction** and move it to the right. Show the player's faction name, color, and flag, or show the creation form there if they have no faction. Add a 6 × 6 grid with a color picker so the player can paint and preview a flag when creating or editing their faction. Validate the 36 cells on the server and share the saved flag for use in province panels. Use a solid tile in the faction color for factions without a custom flag.
4. **Start or join fights from the province panel.** Declare an attack and enter the arena immediately from the same action. Remove the confirmation and gathering delay for this flow. Give faction founders the same action on a double-click, subject to the server's eligibility check. In a contested province, put the defender first and each attacking faction on its own row underneath. Show each side's flag and name and offer **Join** where the player is eligible. If the province is unowned, mark it as unclaimed and list the attackers.
5. **Build province-shaped arenas.** Scale the selected province's outline up proportionally for the playable arena. Use the world screen's square viewport for the battle view. Show the province name and **Return to world map** in the left panel where the player entered. Put the minimap in the right panel in place of **Faction** and track the local player with a red dot. Place defenders near the central capture point.
6. **Update the battle controls and add art.** Put the local player's health bar in the top-left of the battle viewport and show it at all times, including at zero while waiting to respawn. Press Space to pick up the nearest eligible ground item in range, using the same equipment rules as click-to-pick-up. Add pixel-art player sprites with idle and movement animations, terrain tiles, bullet sprites, and an AK-47 sprite that serves as the template for future items. Place trees and shrubs to break up open areas, with solid cover that blocks movement and projectiles.
7. **Verify the full loop.** Use separate browser sessions for the defender and two attacking factions. Paint flags, declare and enter a fight from the map, join both kinds of side, capture the province, and verify the ownership update in each session. Check the matching world and battle viewport sizes, minimap marker, cover, health bar, and pickup by Space. Use focused Vitest checks for border adjacency, spawn placement (including a landless attacker when every neighbor is owned), attack retries, competing pickup requests, and ownership updates.

## Province geometry and names

- Export each shared border as one curve used by both provinces, with fixed junctions where borders meet. Use the same contours for map fills, outlines, and hit areas. Verify that provinces still meet cleanly at shared borders and that shorelines and land adjacency match the selected map.
- Use these names in existing province ID order, from `province-01` through `province-10`. Flintwatch, Rillmark, Alderreach, Dunmere, Gullstrand, Tarnwick, Saltvenn, Greyfen, Brackenholt, and High Tor.
- Use the same province geometry to define arena bounds, the minimap, and eligible spawn edges. Use the v0.2 arena as the size reference. Keep movement, drops, and projectile collisions inside the playable area. Use the same collision geometry for client prediction and server simulation.

## Entering a battle and choosing a spawn

- A founder who double-clicks a province enters on their faction's side if it owns the province or is already attacking. Otherwise, the double-click declares an attack and enters if the faction is eligible. Players without a faction can use the side buttons in a contested province. Joining a side leaves the player's world-map faction membership intact.
- Validate eligibility, record the attack, and reserve entry on the server. A retry after a dropped response should let the player finish joining the same battle. A capture updates the battle result and province ownership together, with at most one owning faction.
- If the attacker owns one neighboring province, use it as the origin. If it owns several, choose the one with the longest usable shared border so entry stays immediate. Record the origin with the attack and pick a random clear spawn just inside that shared edge.
- For a landless attacker, pick a random clear spawn along any edge of the playable arena, regardless of who owns the neighboring provinces. A displaced defender without a shared-border origin spawns along a clear part of the perimeter.
- Use the same spawn area for each faction's arrivals and respawns in the battle. Keep cover clear of spawn areas and routes to the capture point.

## Item pickup

Resolve Space pickup on the server from the player's current position. Pickup requires a living player with an empty equipment slot. When two players reach for one item, only one can receive it.

## Later work

Add climate maps, PostgreSQL persistence, and daily ownership snapshots with a history book. Author more detailed terrain layouts once the province-shaped arenas have been playtested.
