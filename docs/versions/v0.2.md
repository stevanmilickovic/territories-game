# v0.2 plan

The goal is to play the territory capture loop on a generated map of ten continental territories. Players choose how to enter, interact directly with the world map, and fight in a larger arena with visible projectiles.

## Steps

1. **Generate the continent and territories.** Build a seeded elevation map from layered Perlin noise. Keep a mostly continental region with bodies of water determined by elevation. Place ten seeded territory centers across the mainland and give each a seeded random strength. Use terrain-weighted borders, tune them in a preview, and select one generated map for the first playtest.
2. **Add a player entry screen.** Offer **Play as guest** and **Choose a username** before showing the world map. The server assigns guest names and validates custom usernames. Use the chosen display name for the session and show it above the player in the arena.
3. **Make the world map interactive.** Center a square map in the largest area the browser can fit and overlay the existing controls in its corners. Fill owned territories with their faction's color and update the fill when ownership changes. Selecting a territory shows its owner and the available actions. Require confirmation for a new attack and show joinable sides for an active contest.
4. **Enlarge the arena and add projectiles.** Start with an arena three times wider and taller than v0.1, from 800 × 480 to 2400 × 1440 world units. Keep a square viewport and have the camera smoothly follow the locally predicted player. Convert mouse aim through the camera so it stays aligned with the world as the player moves. Draw fast square projectiles, show local firing feedback immediately, and have the server simulate projectile travel, collisions, and damage. Check for hits along the path a projectile travels each tick.
5. **Add weapon items and contextual health.** Make the AK-47 an equipped item that can become a ground item when a player drops it or is eliminated. Add the weapon slot and ground-item interactions. Show the local player's health bar beneath their character on hover or briefly after taking a hit.
6. **Verify the full loop.** Use two independent browser sessions to try guest entry and a custom username, declare or join a contest from the map, and fight in the same arena. Check player labels, camera tracking, projectile hits, respawns, item drops and pickups, and health-bar visibility. Capture a territory and confirm both world maps show it in the new owner's color. Repeat combat under simulated latency and check that the local aim direction follows the pointer immediately, including while the camera moves. Fix aim prediction only if the check reveals a delay or mismatch.

## Map generation

- Use base frequency to control the size of major terrain features, lacunarity for the frequency change between noise layers, and persistence for the amount of fine detail. Use sea level to form water from low terrain and allow land to reach the map edges for future expansion. Territory boundaries follow shorelines and shared borders with neighbors. Where land reaches the edge of the map, the view clips the territory there.
- Space the ten centers so they start on land and have room to grow. On flat terrain, assign each location to the center with the lowest distance divided by strength. Centers with strengths 10 and 5 meet two-thirds of the way from the stronger one toward the weaker one.
- Compute terrain-weighted distance across land. Raise the cost of crossing prominent ridges and drainage channels so borders tend to follow those features. Use a smoothed heightmap for border generation and tune ridge and drainage influence independently.
- Preview elevation, centers, and borders across several seeds. Tune the scale of terrain features before adjusting border sensitivity. Verify that each territory is connected to its center, each land cell belongs to exactly one territory, and adjacency comes from shared land borders. Use the selected map and stable territory IDs on both the client and server.

### Selected map

- Use seed `202` on a 160 × 160 grid with base frequency 1.5, four elevation layers, lacunarity 2, persistence 0.24, and sea level 0.472.
- Keep the ten centers from the two-layer preview of the same seed and grid. Center placement uses a minimum spacing of 31 cells and strengths from 0.86 to 1.14.
- Assign territories on the four-layer terrain using ridge influence 70, drainage influence 105, and a smoothing radius of 2 cells.

## Joining a side

For the prototype, let guests without a faction join an existing side for the arena session. Players with a faction enter on their own side when eligible. The server validates the choice and holds it for the arena session. Display names are session-only.

## Arena items and health

- The AK-47 is the first item type. Players spawn with one equipped in their single weapon slot. Leaving that slot empty prevents firing until they pick up a weapon.
- Put the weapon slot in a box at the bottom center of the arena viewport. Dragging the weapon out of the box drops it at the player's position. An eliminated player drops their equipped weapon where they fell.
- Give ground items a faint outline within pickup range and a stronger outline when hovered within that range. Clicking a nearby item equips it if the player's slot is empty. The server validates pickup range and transfers the ground item to one player only.
- Show the local player's health bar directly beneath their character while the pointer is over them or briefly after they take damage. Hide it otherwise.
